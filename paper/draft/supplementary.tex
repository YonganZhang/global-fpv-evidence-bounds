\subsection*{Supplementary interpretation rules}

The reference inventory is an assessment scope, not an enumeration of every inland water body worldwide. ``Lower'' and ``upper'' denote deterministic evidence policies. They do not denote confidence limits. Complete L2 under the published 1991--2020 criterion and complete deployable L3 remain unavailable. Exact values below are generated from the validated water-body table; main-text energy values are rounded to the nearest TWh.
