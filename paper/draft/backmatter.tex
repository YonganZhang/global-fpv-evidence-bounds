\section*{Data availability}
The assessment uses publicly accessible or registered source datasets from HydroLAKES, GeoDAR, GRanD, NASA POWER, LI-CCR, the lake FPV dataset of Woolway et al., WDPA, Ramsar, LandScan, GLEV, GRIP, the World Bank distribution of OpenStreetMap power lines, GLOBathy and Natural Earth. Dataset versions and interpretation boundaries are provided in Methods and Supplementary Table~S1. Aggregate result tables, figure-ready outputs, a licence-compatible 199{,}976-row processed table and a separately licensed ODbL power-line derivative are available in release \texttt{v1.0.2-v118} at \url{https://github.com/YonganZhang/global-fpv-evidence-bounds}. WDPA- and LandScan-derived row-level fields are not redistributed because the upstream terms do not permit a blanket open-data relicence; the excluded fields, official acquisition routes and complete reconstruction code are documented in the repository.

\section*{Code availability}
Versioned Python workflows reproduce inventory reconciliation, generation, evidence gates, sensitivity analyses, validation tables and figures. The complete analysis code, environment lock, validation gate and active manuscript sources are available under the MIT licence in release \texttt{v1.0.2-v118} at \url{https://github.com/YonganZhang/global-fpv-evidence-bounds}. Third-party inputs retain their original licences and access conditions.
